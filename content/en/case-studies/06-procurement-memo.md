# Case study 6: Procurement decision memo

<div class="synthetic-label"><strong>SYNTHETIC</strong> Fictional procurement scenario for training only.</div>

## Scenario

A manager used AI to draft a memo recommending a solar equipment vendor for emergency deployment.

**AI memo claims:** "Vendor SolBright has ISO 9001 certification, delivered 500 units to UN agencies in 2025, and offers 48-hour delivery to Region Y."

**Your records:** SolBright is on the approved vendor list but ISO status unverified. No record of UN deliveries. Standard lead time is 14 days.

## Your task (10 minutes)

1. Which claims must be verified before procurement proceeds?
2. How would you mark each claim?
3. Green, amber, or red for using the memo in a approval workflow?
4. Who must sign procurement decisions?

## Model answer

| Claim | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| ISO 9001 | Unverified | Request certificate from vendor |
| UN deliveries 2025 | Unsupported | Remove or verify with references |
| 48-hour delivery | Contradicts internal records | Confirm contract terms |

**Decision:** Amber until verified. Red if memo is submitted as fact without checks.

**Accountability:** Named procurement officer and delegated authority per organizational policy. AI does not sign.

## Common errors

- Trusting "sounds official" vendor credentials
- Skipping verification because of emergency pressure
- Letting AI rank vendors without disclosed criteria

## Principle taught

**Procurement integrity:** AI can fabricate credentials and track records. Verification protects funds and affected populations depending on equipment.
