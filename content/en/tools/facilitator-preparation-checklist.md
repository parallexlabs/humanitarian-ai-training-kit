# Facilitator preparation checklist

Complete at least **five business days** before Session 1. Check each box.

## Hosting organization alignment

- [ ] Named executive sponsor and primary contact for policy questions
- [ ] Safeguarding, PSEA, and protection focal points identified with current contact details
- [ ] Privacy or IT contact identified for amber and red escalation examples
- [ ] Confirmed: no real operational data in exercises
- [ ] Confirmed: classroom role play is not consultation with affected people

## Materials and access

- [ ] Sent [invitation and agenda email](sample-invitation-agenda-email.md) with dates, links, and ground rules
- [ ] Distributed [participant handbook](participant-handbook.md) and worksheets (editable files or paper)
- [ ] Emailed case text as plain text 48 hours before each session (low-bandwidth route)
- [ ] Pre/post assessment forms ready with anonymous participant codes
- [ ] Answer keys printed or offline; not shared with participants before debrief

## Facilitator readiness

- [ ] Read [facilitator delivery guide](facilitator-guide.md) for all sessions you will lead
- [ ] Read run sheets and speaker notes for Sessions 1 to 3 (and clinic if applicable)
- [ ] Review [safeguarding module](../sessions/session-03-governance/safeguarding-module.md)
- [ ] Practised timing once with co-facilitator or colleague (90-minute dry run recommended)
- [ ] Co-facilitator assigned for groups above 30 participants

## Technology

- [ ] Completed [technology and accessibility checklist](technology-accessibility-checklist.md)
- [ ] Tested breakout rooms, chat, polls, and phone dial-in
- [ ] Captioning plan confirmed (live CART, platform captions, or equivalent)
- [ ] Backup plenary-only route tested
- [ ] Recording policy communicated (default: do not record breakouts)

## Assessment

- [ ] Rubric scoring sheet ready for performance scenarios and worksheets
- [ ] Plan for secure storage of anonymous assessment data per org policy
- [ ] Sample final-report page shared with sponsor as reporting template only (see assessment folder)

## Optional routes

- [ ] If using [live-tool lab](../sessions/session-02-verified-work/live-tool-lab-route.md): written org approval, synthetic data pack only, safety briefing scripted
- [ ] If bilingual delivery: French materials pre-sent; interpretation plan confirmed

## Day before Session 1

- [ ] Reminder email with link, dial-in, and accommodation contacts
- [ ] Slide deck and run sheet open offline
- [ ] Safeguarding escalation card visible to co-host

## After each session

- [ ] Worksheets collected or received within 48 hours
- [ ] Themes for hosting org (no individual ranking) drafted within 3 business days
- [ ] Incidents or disclosures referred per org policy and documented without sensitive detail
