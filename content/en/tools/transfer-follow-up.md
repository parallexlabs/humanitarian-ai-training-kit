# 30-day workplace transfer follow-up

**Illustrative training-design sample. Not a record of delivered work.** Self-reported behaviour only; not observed workplace practice.

## Purpose

This form supports **Kirkpatrick level 3 (behaviour)**: whether participants report applying kit skills at work about 30 days after the final session. Results are self-reported transfer, not observed behaviour.

**Reference:** Kirkpatrick, J. D., & Kirkpatrick, W. K. (2016). [*Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Training Evaluation*](https://www.td.org/product/book--kirkpatricks-four-levels-of-training-evaluation/111614). ATD Press.

## When to send

- **Timing:** 30 calendar days after the final session (Session 3 or clinic, whichever ends the series).
- **Reminders:** For anonymous collection, send one general reminder to all invitees after seven days, asking those who already responded to disregard it. Use individual reminders only when participants consented to identifiable response tracking. Do not chase further.
- **Anonymous option:** Offer a link or paper form with no name field. If you track codes, use participant-held codes only; do not link to performance scores without consent.

## Analysis and reporting

1. **Count responses** and report the **response rate** (responses received divided by eligible participants).
2. **Report response distributions and valid n for each item.** Report N/A and missing responses separately. Do not calculate item means or a combined transfer score; frequency is not proficiency.
3. **Always label results** as **self-reported transfer, not observed behaviour**.
4. **Do not rank individuals** or publish identifiable quotes.
5. **Feed a short summary** into the [sample final-report page](../assessment/sample-final-report-page.md) (transfer section).

**Level 4 (organizational results)** is outside this kit's scope; your organization may track outcomes separately with its own governance.
