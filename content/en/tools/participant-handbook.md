# Participant handbook

**Learning materials only.** For staff joining the three-session series and optional clinic.

## What this training is

- Three 90-minute sessions plus an optional 60-minute follow-up clinic
- Synthetic case studies only; no real beneficiary or operational records in exercises
- Skills focus: screen tasks, verify outputs, protect data, and draft bounded pilot charters
- Not legal advice; follow your organization's policies and counsel

## What you need

| Item | Required? | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Paid AI account | No | Default route uses pre-supplied outputs |
| Camera | No | Typed chat participation is equivalent |
| Stable video | No | Phone plus chat route available (see technology checklist) |
| Pre-reading | Optional | [AI use decision guide](ai-decision-guide.md) recommended |

## Session overview

| Session | You will practice |
|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | Seven-step screen; green, amber, red decisions; SMS translation case |
| 2 | Structured prompts; source-check tables; role-based workflow choices |
| 3 | Pilot charter; transparency card exercise; governance options; safeguarding and protection lens |
| Clinic | De-identified workplace questions only |

## Your responsibilities

1. **Protect data:** Do not paste real names, case numbers, locations, or donor details into chat, worksheets, or any AI tool during the series.
2. **Verify before action:** Classroom decisions do not authorize operational use. A named approver at your organization must approve real work.
3. **Speak up:** If you cannot access breakouts, captions, or materials, tell the host before Session 1.
4. **Safeguarding:** If you need to discuss a disclosure of harm or child protection concern, contact your organization's focal point. Facilitators will not investigate in plenary.

## Assessment

- **Before Session 1:** Pre-assessment (Kirkpatrick level 2 learning; performance scenarios plus short confidence items)
- **After Session 3:** Post-assessment (different examples, same skills)
- **After each session:** Optional session evaluation (Kirkpatrick level 1 reaction)
- **About 30 days after the series:** Optional [transfer follow-up](transfer-follow-up.md) (Kirkpatrick level 3 behaviour, self-reported)
- **During sessions:** Worksheets scored with a four-point rubric
- Results are reported in aggregate. Small groups (15 to 40) cannot support reliable subgroup analysis. Level 4 (organizational results) is outside this kit's scope.

- Try one screening or verification step on an approved, low-risk task at work
- Bring de-identified questions to the clinic
- If your organization adapts this kit, affected-community representatives should be involved in that adaptation process (see [AAP design note](aap-adaptation-design-note.md))
