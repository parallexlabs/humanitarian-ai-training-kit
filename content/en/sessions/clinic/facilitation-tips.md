# Clinic facilitation tips

## Low attendance

Merge with async FAQ document sent to all registrants.

## High sensitivity questions

Answer with generic pattern: "When registration data and an unapproved vendor..." without repeating details from the question.

## French participants

Accept written FR questions; respond in English unless host provides interpretation.

## Documentation

Facilitator sends hosting org a one-page themes summary. No verbatim quotes that could identify participants or orgs.
