# Follow-up clinic learning objectives

**60 minutes** | Approximately one month after Session 3

## Purpose

Reinforce workplace application, answer de-identified questions, and share lessons across organizations without exposing sensitive data.

## By the end, participants will be able to:

1. **Apply** the seven-step screen to a question from their own work context (described without identifiers).
2. **Distinguish** questions that require organizational policy answers from those solvable with verification habits.
3. **Identify** one barrier that stopped them from applying learning since Session 3.
4. **Commit** to one next step with a named internal contact where possible.

## Not objectives

- Resolve organization-specific legal questions (refer to counsel)
- Review real case files or beneficiary data
- Endorse specific vendors or tools
