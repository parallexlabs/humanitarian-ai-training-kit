# Session 3 speaker notes

## Teach block

Use examples from participants' org types (international NGO, domestic charity, faith-based, multilateral affiliate). Do not name real vendors from the room.

## Decision block

Most groups should land on option 2 or a heavily bounded option 3. Full green adoption should be rare.

Push groups to fill charter fields even if decision is "no AI."

## Challenge block

Assign challenge groups randomly. Require revision, not defense only.

Watch for performative "we consulted communities" language. Correct gently: exercise is fictional.

## Post-assessment

Allow 10 minutes after session if hosting organization wants same-day completion.

## Close

Promote clinic: questions must be de-identified. No case numbers or beneficiary details.
