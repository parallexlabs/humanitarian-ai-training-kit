# Session 3 facilitation tips

## Sensitive topics

Governance discussions can surface fear of job loss or embarrassment about past tool use. Ground in systems, not blame.

## Equity

Ask how migrant status, language, disability, or documentation might change AI outcomes. Fictional brief should prompt subgroup checks in charter.

## Leadership participants

Invite them to name realistic approval paths. Avoid letting them dominate airtime.

## Low-bandwidth

Charter template works offline. Challenge block can be async: Group A posts decision; Group B posts written challenge in forum.

## Materials handoff

Ensure hosting organization receives pilot charter templates and aggregate themes for communities of practice.
