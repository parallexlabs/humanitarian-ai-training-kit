# Individual convenience vs policy

| Situation | Risk |
|-----------|------|
| Free tool for meeting notes | May violate data policy |
| Approved tool for public text | May be green with checks |
| Auto-approving assistance | Requires governance |

Policy protects people, not only IT.

---

# Green / amber / red at org level

- **Green:** Approved use case, approved tool, verification built in
- **Amber:** Pilot with charter, limited data, executive sponsor
- **Red:** No framework, sensitive data, auto-decisions on rights

---

# Decision options today

1. **No AI** until framework exists
2. **More evidence** and real consultation
3. **Limited pilot** for leadership review

No live data in the classroom exercise.

---

# Safeguarding and protection

- Child protection and GBV-related data: default **red** in unapproved tools
- Disclosures in training: pause, refer to org focal points, do not investigate in plenary
- PSEA reporting routes must be known before incidents
- See safeguarding module and data-responsibility checklist

---

# Pilot charter (essentials)

- Task and non-AI baseline
- Owner and sponsor
- Permitted inputs and tool
- Consultation still required
- Success, failure, and subgroup checks
- Human approval and remedy
- Manual fallback and safe exit
