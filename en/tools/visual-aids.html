AI use decision flow

Text equivalent:

  1. Purpose: What task does AI support? What is the manual alternative?
  2. People: Who is affected? Was consultation adequate for decisions affecting them?
  3. Data: What data will you enter? Is the tool approved for that data?
  4. Tool: Where does input go? Training use? Third-party access?
  5. Verification: Which claims need a trusted source? How will you check?
  6. Human control: Who approves, overrides, and provides remedy?
  7. Monitoring: What is the test period? What triggers a stop?
  8. Decision: Record green (proceed with checks), amber (pause for review), or red (stop) with a named owner and review date.

See also: AI use decision guide.

Data responsibility lifecycle

Text equivalent (IASC programme cycle):

Stage Question before using AI on operational data
Plan Is AI necessary? Who is accountable?
Assess What harms could data or AI outputs cause?
Collect Is collection limited, lawful, and explained to people?
Share Are sharing agreements and purpose limits clear?
Analyse Could analysis re-identify people or mislead decision-makers?
Store Retention, access control, and breach response defined?
Close When and how is data deleted or returned?

See also: Data-responsibility checklist and IASC Operational Guidance (2023).

Safeguarding escalation (session use)

Text equivalent:

Step Action
1 Pause exercise when real sensitive information appears
2 Remove identifying content from chat per platform rules
3 Refer participant to org safeguarding, PSEA, or protection focal point
4 Do not investigate in plenary
5 Notify hosting org contact after session without copying sensitive detail
