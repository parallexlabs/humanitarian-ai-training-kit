AI use decision guide

One page for quick reference. Use with your organization’s policies. Classroom use is not consultation with affected people or partners.

Text equivalent: Purpose, people, data, tool, verification, human control, monitoring, then record green, amber, or red with an owner. Full table in visual aids.

Seven questions before you use AI

Step Question Stop if…
1. Purpose What task does AI support? What is the manual alternative? AI adds no value or a manual method is safer
2. People Who is affected? Were affected people and local partners consulted? A decision affecting communities was not consulted
3. Data What data will you enter? Is the tool approved for that data? Personal or sensitive data in an unapproved tool
4. Tool Where does input go? Who can access it? Is it used for training? Tool is unapproved or terms are unclear
5. Verification Which claims need a trusted source? How will you check? No way to verify consequential outputs
6. Human control Who approves? Who can override? What is the remedy? No named human accountable for the decision
7. Monitoring What is the test period? What triggers a stop? No failure threshold or incident route

Traffic-light decision

Green: proceed with checks

  • Low-risk, already-approved use
  • Only permitted public or internal data
  • A named person will verify outputs before action
  • Source checking is planned

Amber: pause for review

  • Privacy, protection, or partner review needed
  • Tool approval unclear
  • Outputs affect access to aid, safety, or rights
  • Consultation incomplete

Red: stop

  • Prohibited input (personal or sensitive data in wrong tool)
  • Unapproved tool for this data type
  • High-stakes decision with no human review path
  • No safe remedy if the output is wrong

Record your decision

Write one line: Decision (G/A/R), owner name, next review date.

Stop if the source is unverified or a human approval point is missing.
