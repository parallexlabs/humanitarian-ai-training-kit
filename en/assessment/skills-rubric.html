Skills rubric for practical exercises

Use during Sessions 1 to 3 case practice. Award one point per criterion (maximum 4 per exercise).

Criteria (all sessions)

# Criterion 1 point when… 0 points when…
1 Input/use decision Appropriate green, amber, or red with a valid reason Wrong classification or no reason
2 Source verification At least one consequential claim checked against an authorized source No check or wrong source
3 Bias or omission Identifies exclusion, harmful omission, or stereotype Misses a taught error in the case
4 Human control Names approver, override path, or stop rule No human accountability point

Proficiency: 4/4 on an exercise. Series target: at least 8 in 10 participants who complete both entry and exit checks score 4/4 on the exit exercise (organizational reporting choice).

Exercise variants

Each session includes:

  • One low-risk task where green (with checks) is correct
  • One must-stop task where red is correct

Rejecting all AI use is not proficiency. Participants must distinguish appropriate use from prohibited use.

Facilitator scoring notes

  • Score from the participant worksheet, not from eloquence in plenary
  • Partial credit is not awarded; use 0 or 1 per row
  • If a group disagrees, record the majority decision and note dissent in debrief
  • Do not rank individuals or organizations in aggregate reports
