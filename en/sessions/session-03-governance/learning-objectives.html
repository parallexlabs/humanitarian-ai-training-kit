Session 3 learning objectives

Level 3: Governance and adoption | 90 minutes

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

  1. Compare individual convenience uses with organizational policy and classify examples as green, amber, or red.
  2. Analyze a fictional organizational AI challenge using a governance lens (no real operational data).
  3. Choose among no AI, more evidence and consultation, or a limited pilot for organizational review.
  4. Challenge another group’s decision from frontline, manager, and policy perspectives.
  5. Revise a decision with a named approval owner and safe next action.
  6. Draft key fields of a bounded pilot charter (owner, baseline, inputs, success, exit).

Observable behaviours

  • Records a governance decision with consultation gaps named explicitly
  • Identifies at least two stakeholder perspectives that change the recommendation
  • Names an approval owner and stop threshold
  • States that classroom exercise is not community consultation
