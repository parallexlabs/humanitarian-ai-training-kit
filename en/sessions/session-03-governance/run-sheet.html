Session 3 facilitator run sheet

Governance and adoption | 90 minutes | Level 3

Time Activity Facilitator actions Materials
0:00 to 0:10 Recall Link sessions 1 to 2 to governance Slide 2
0:10 to 0:25 Teach: policy vs convenience; safeguarding module (10 min) Green/amber/red examples; child protection, GBV data, PSEA escalation Slides 3 to 6; safeguarding-module.md
0:25 to 0:55 Decision block Groups use fictional brief (vendor pitch or data-sharing) Case 7 or 8; charter template
0:55 to 1:25 Challenge block Groups swap; challenge from 3 perspectives; revise Challenge cards
1:25 to 1:30 Close Post-assessment reminder; action back to work Slide 11

Fictional brief (supplied)

“Organization is offered an AI analytics platform for registration data. Vendor promises faster gap analysis. No AI framework exists yet.”

No personal or sensitive operational data may be entered in the exercise.

Challenge perspectives (cards)

  1. Frontline staff: workload, accuracy, beneficiary trust
  2. Manager: cost, liability, partner relations
  3. Policy/privacy: legal basis, data sharing, exit plan
