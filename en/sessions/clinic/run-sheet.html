Follow-up clinic facilitator run sheet

60 minutes | Virtual recommended

Time Activity Notes
0:00 to 0:05 Welcome and rules De-identified questions only; facilitator may pause if PII appears
0:05 to 0:15 Pulse poll What did you try? What stopped you? (anonymous poll)
0:15 to 0:45 Q&A queue Apply screen to 3 to 5 pre-submitted questions; co-host reads chat
0:45 to 0:55 Peer exchange Volunteers share one success and one barrier (no org shaming)
0:55 to 1:00 Close Evaluation link; remind materials stay open-licence

Pre-submission form (hosting org)

Collect questions 1 week before. Strip identifiers before sharing with facilitator.

If no questions submitted

Use backup scenarios: Case 6 procurement, Case 8 vendor pitch, or charter review of a fictional pilot.
