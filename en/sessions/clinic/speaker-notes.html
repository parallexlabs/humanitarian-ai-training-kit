Clinic speaker notes

Keep answers short. Map every question to the seven-step screen and G/A/R.

When legal interpretation is requested, say: “Your privacy lead or counsel decides; here is what the screen would flag.”

Celebrate small wins (one verified statistic, one stopped paste of PII).

Do not promise future products or client services.

If attendance is low, run as structured discussion of pulse poll themes.
