Session 1 facilitation tips

Low-bandwidth settings

  • Email case text and worksheet 48 hours before the session
  • Use plenary discussion instead of breakouts if connections drop
  • Accept worksheet submissions by email within 24 hours
  • Avoid video-heavy slides; all content is text-based

Inclusion

  • Offer French materials in advance; live facilitation may be English with written FR questions welcomed
  • Describe any diagram verbally
  • Repeat chat contributions aloud for audio-only participants
  • No-breakout route: facilitator reads case; individuals complete worksheet; debrief in plenary

Difficult moments

Participant shares real case data: Pause activity. Ask co-host to remove from chat per platform rules. Refer privately to hosting organization’s contact. Do not copy into notes.

“AI is always dangerous”: Acknowledge risk. Redirect to screen: some green uses exist with checks. Proficiency requires discrimination, not blanket refusal.

“Our org has no policy”: Amber by default. Action plan: ask leadership for guidance. Session 3 addresses governance.

Pacing for large groups (35 to 40)

  • Use 5 groups of 7 to 8; pairs within groups for worksheet sections
  • Collect G/A/R via poll tool instead of lengthy verbal reports
  • Pre-assign group numbers in registration email

Evidence of learning

  • Collect worksheets (anonymous codes only)
  • Score using four-point rubric
  • Note themes for adaptation note to hosting organization within 3 business days
