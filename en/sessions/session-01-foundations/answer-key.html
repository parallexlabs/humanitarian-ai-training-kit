Session 1 answer key and debrief questions

Part A answers

Draft statement W1 check Action
Road B is open Not established Remove; ask authorized local source
All entrances are step-free Contradicted Correct; only Entrance E is step-free
Go directly there now Outside reviewer authority Stop; use alert approval process

Part B guidance

Step Expected themes
1 Review draft only; manual route is established alert service
2 People using wheelchairs harmed by wrong accessibility info; route errors
3 Input is fictional bulletin only; no personal data in exercise
4 If tool used for translation, must be approved; terms matter
5 Each line checked against W1
6 Local communications lead approves; reviewer cannot send
7 Stop on contradiction; incident route per org policy

Part C model answer

  • Decision: Red
  • Owner: Authorized local communications lead
  • Protection issue: False accessibility information excludes people who need step-free access
  • Manual fallback: Established manual alert process

Debrief questions

  1. Why is “sounds urgent” not a reason to send?
  2. How is a translation error different from a policy violation?
  3. What would change your decision from red to amber?
  4. Who in your organization would you escalate to?

Rubric scoring (4 points)

Criterion Look for
Input/use decision Red with valid reason
Source verification Completed table with contradiction flagged
Bias/omission Accessibility or route harm identified
Human control Named approver + manual fallback
