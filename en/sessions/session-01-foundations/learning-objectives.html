Session 1 learning objectives

Level 1: Responsible AI foundations | 90 minutes

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

  1. Distinguish generative AI from prediction and automation tools, and state what each can and cannot do in humanitarian work.
  2. Explain who remains accountable when AI supports a humanitarian decision.
  3. Compare an AI-assisted draft to an authorized source and mark claims as supported, contradicted, or not established.
  4. Apply a seven-step risk screen to a fictional case and record a green, amber, or red decision with a named owner.
  5. Identify at least one protection or localization issue in a synthetic communications scenario.
  6. Choose one safe action to apply before the next session.

Observable behaviours (assessment alignment)

Objective Observable behaviour
Distinguish AI types Correctly classifies an example tool as generative, predictive, or automation in a recall question
Accountability Names a human decision owner, not the tool vendor
Source comparison Completes a source-check table with at least one contradiction flagged
Risk screen Records G/A/R with a reason tied to data, verification, or control
Protection Describes who could be harmed by a wrong message or omission
Action plan Writes one specific, low-risk next step
