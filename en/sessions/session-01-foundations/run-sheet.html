Session 1 facilitator run sheet

Responsible AI foundations | 90 minutes | Level 1

Time Activity Facilitator actions Materials
0:00 to 0:10 Opening and diagnostic Welcome; ground rules (no real case data in chat; cameras optional); four recall questions Slides 1 to 3; diagnostic handout
0:10 to 0:25 Teach: AI types and accountability Explain generative vs predictive vs automation; who is accountable; limitations Slides 4 to 7; speaker notes
0:25 to 0:45 Worked example: source check Walk through draft vs bulletin; model marking supported/contradicted/not established Slides 8 to 10; Case 01 excerpt
0:45 to 1:10 Group exercise: evacuation SMS Groups apply seven-step screen; assign reader, recorder, risk challenger, source checker Worksheet; Case 01 full
1:10 to 1:25 Debrief Each group reports one protection/localization issue and G/A/R; facilitator consolidates Answer key
1:25 to 1:30 Close One-rule recall; each participant writes one action Slide 14; evaluation form optional

Roles in groups (rotate between sessions)

  • Reader: reads source aloud or in chat
  • Recorder: fills worksheet
  • Risk challenger: argues for amber/red
  • Source checker: tracks evidence column

Timing buffers

  • If behind at 0:40: shorten teach block by 5 minutes; keep group exercise
  • If ahead at 1:15: optional pair share of action plans

Accessibility checks

  • Describe visuals on slides
  • Accept typed responses in chat as equivalent to spoken reports
  • Offer plenary-only route if breakouts are inaccessible
