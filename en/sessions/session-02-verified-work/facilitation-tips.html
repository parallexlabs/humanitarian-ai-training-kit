Session 2 facilitation tips

No AI account route (default)

All outputs are pre-supplied on the worksheet. Participants never need to log into an AI tool.

Optional live-tool lab

Organizations with a written approved tool may use the live-tool lab route. Synthetic data only. Participants without accounts stay on the pre-supplied route.

Low-bandwidth

  • Send Case 2 source and AI summary as plain text files before the session
  • Synchronous route: phone plus chat; no breakouts if unstable; co-host collects typed answers
  • Async pair work only when live join is impossible: partners comment within 48 hours, debrief live

Common confusions

  • “I’ll fix the prompt and rerun” in classroom: OK for learning; at work requires approved tool and data rules
  • Rounding 31% to 30% is not the same error as inventing 78%
  • Managers skipping exercise: Insist on workflow decision; it is often the highest-value output

Accessibility

  • Source-check table works in Word, Google Docs, or paper
  • Color is not used to mark supported/contradicted; words only

Adaptation after Session 1

If diagnostic showed weak source-check skills, extend group exercise by 5 minutes and shorten prompt pair work.
