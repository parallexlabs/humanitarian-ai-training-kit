Case study 3: Donor report draft

SYNTHETIC Fictional project data for training only.

Scenario

A program officer used an approved internal AI tool to draft a quarterly donor narrative from approved public annexes. You review before submission.

Annex A (authorized): “Q1 outputs: 1,240 hygiene kits distributed in Region X. Partner: Local NGO Beta. No cash transfers in Q1.”

AI draft excerpt: “In Q1, the project delivered 1,240 hygiene kits and 320 unconditional cash grants to 320 families in Region X, in partnership with Local NGO Alpha.”

Your task (10 minutes)

  1. List each factual claim in the excerpt.
  2. Verify against Annex A.
  3. What is your decision on submitting as-is?
  4. What process should follow?

Model answer

Claim Annex A Issue
1,240 hygiene kits Supported OK with source cite
320 cash grants Not in Annex A Invented
Partner Alpha Annex says Beta Wrong partner
320 families Not in Annex A Invented

Decision: Red for submission. Rewrite from annexes. Human program lead signs after verification.

Common errors

  • Assuming the tool had access to files the reviewer did not check
  • Fixing only one error and missing partner name swap
  • Submitting because “the tone is donor-friendly”

Principle taught

Donor accountability: Narrative drafts require line-by-line verification. Invented outputs undermine trust and compliance.
