Case study 4: Protection case note containing personal data

SYNTHETIC Fictional case note. No real person is described.

Scenario

A caseworker asks whether they can paste a protection case note into a free online AI tool to “make it shorter for the file.”

Fictional case note excerpt: “Client A., female, age 17, resides at Shelter Line 3. Phone: 555-0142. Reported assault on [date]. Perpetrator described as…”

Your task (8 minutes)

  1. Classify the data in the excerpt.
  2. Green, amber, or red for pasting into a free public AI tool?
  3. What safer alternatives exist?
  4. What if the caseworker already pasted it?

Model answer

Data classification: Personal and sensitive protection data. Likely special category under many data-protection frameworks.

Decision: Red. Do not paste into unapproved public tools.

Safer alternatives:

  • Use an organization-approved tool with a data-processing agreement, if policy allows
  • Summarize manually without identifiers
  • Use internal templates approved by protection and privacy leads

If already pasted: Stop use, report to privacy/protection lead same day, follow incident procedure. Do not copy AI output back into the case file without review.

Common errors

  • “I’ll remove the name” while leaving phone or shelter line
  • Assuming deletion from chat erases vendor-side retention
  • Treating shortening as low risk when re-identification is possible

Principle taught

Data minimization and approved tools: Protection data rarely belongs in public AI tools. Policy and incident routes override convenience.
